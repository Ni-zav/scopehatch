**Clock conventions:** record real start/end with timezone, exclude paused/break periods and describe exclusions. Report both unassisted completion rate and *assisted* completion separately. `not tested` must not enter a successful-task denominator. If interrupted/abandoned, keep elapsed time and cause; do not set duration to zero. Exports and approvals that should be refused are **successfully blocked**, not failed completions.
