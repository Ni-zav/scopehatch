# Business validation before charging

Interview 5–10 actual operators in archviz/design/software services. Ask for anonymized recent briefs, existing rate-card form, review process, cost of underquoting, requirements most often missed, and who signs proposals. Do not invent willingness to pay.

An unpaid pilot should test 10+ real-ish briefs with explicit permission. Record extraction accuracy, cost-estimate corrections, review time, usability complaints, number of completed quote exports, false commitments, support time and repeat weekly usage. Identify source/currency/quantity distinctions.

If clients prefer their current spreadsheet and AI draft tools, prioritize integrations and export rather than feature multiplication. Price managed hosting only when a repeated painful workflow and trust are demonstrated.

Success = users bring their own next briefs without being chased; zero unauthorized approvals; no ungrounded binding prices. Vanity metric = LLM calls performed.
